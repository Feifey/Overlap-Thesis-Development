Unsent Archive

An anonymous public archive of the things people chose not to share at work, and why.

Photo: a wall of anonymous notes, sorted by reason
Goal

Understand what stays behind, and make keeping something to yourself feel normal, not like a gap.

Outcomes so far

Year one: the first open dataset and a reading-room installation.

Activities
  1. 01Anonymous submissions, each with a reason: private, not sure yet, not relevant.
  2. 02A university HCI lab studies the reasons with us.
  3. 03Once a year, the full dataset is released for free.
What we spend

No revenue. Funded by Overlap’s app and Integrations.
