Handoff Atlas

People were handing off work long before AI. A nurse briefs the next shift. A teacher leaves notes for a substitute. We learn how they do it, and bring those lessons to teams working with AI.

Photo: a nurse briefing the next shift
Goal

Learn from jobs that have handled handoffs for decades, and bring that knowledge back into how teams share AI work.

Outcomes so far

Pilot with one hospital unit and one kitchen.

Activities
  1. 01Visiting a hospital shift change, a restaurant kitchen and a school.
  2. 02Collecting the simple habits that make handing off work go well.
  3. 03A free guide any team can use, AI or not.
What we spend

No revenue. Funded by Overlap’s app and Integrations.
