Already shaped by people like you

Two early conversations changed the design.

A RESEARCHER
kept different directions of an AI chat apart, so ideas wouldn’t get mixed up. That became the branch map.
A DESIGNER
imagined a map of what everyone on the team was exploring. That became the team map.
Who we’d love to hear from

Anyone who uses AI and works with a team.

Designers, engineers, researchers, product managers, writers. Your role matters less than your experience.

How we treat what you tell us
YOU CHOOSE WHAT TO TELL US
Share only what you’re comfortable with. Skip any question.
NOTHING CONFIDENTIAL
We never ask for AI sessions, work files, or anything you’re not allowed to share.
ANONYMOUS BY DEFAULT
We remove names and details that could point to you or your workplace.
STOP ANYTIME
You can leave at any point, and nobody at your work will know you took part.
