About

It started with a
clipboard that kept failing.

When something I copied on my phone didn’t show up on my laptop, I became the connection: finding it again, restoring the context, carrying it across by hand.

Then I saw the same thing at work. People carry their AI work into their team by hand, and decide on the way what to leave behind. That deciding is real work. Overlap is built to support it, and to protect what people keep.

Our mission

You decide what to share, and what to keep in your chat.

Photo of Feifey
Team

Feifey

Founder, designer, and right now, the whole team.

Feifey is a second-year MFA Interaction Design student at the School of Visual Arts in New York. Overlap began as her thesis on how people bring AI-assisted work into their teams, and what they choose to keep for themselves.

Built on research

Overlap is shaped by conversations with designers, engineers and researchers about how they bring AI work into their teams, plus a close look at the tools they use every day.
