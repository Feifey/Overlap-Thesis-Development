Integrations

Work where
you already work.

Overlap runs as an add-on inside the tools where you think with AI, and connects to the spaces where your team works. Your session stays in your tool. Only what you share reaches your team.

In your AI tools

Your sessions become a branch map.

CC

Claude Code

Available

Each coding session becomes a branch map. Share the approach that worked, not the whole session.

USE CASE
An engineer shares the fix for a flaky test, with a short note on why it works.
CX

Codex

Beta

Tasks and attempts show up as branches. Pick the attempt worth showing your team.

USE CASE
Compare three attempts, share the one that passed review.
VS

VS Code

Available

A side panel shows your AI chat as a branch map, right next to your code.

USE CASE
Keep exploring privately, share a summary when it’s ready.
FG

Figma

Coming soon

A plugin keeps the AI explorations behind a design as branches.

USE CASE
Share the reasoning behind a layout before a critique.
In your team’s spaces

Shared pieces arrive where your team already talks.

S

Slack

A shared summary arrives as a card, with “ask me” attached.

MT

Microsoft Teams

The same card, in your team’s channel.

N

Notion

Add an approved summary to a doc, linked to the person who wrote it.

L

Linear

Attach the context behind a decision to the ticket.

GH

GitHub

Add the reasoning to a pull request, not the whole AI session.

Same rule in every tool: your session stays in your tool. Only what you share leaves it.
