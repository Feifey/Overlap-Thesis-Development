How it works

Think in branches. Share what’s ready.
Talk to the right person.

01

Branch your thinking

Every question you ask and every answer you get becomes a node. Try a new direction without losing the earlier one, and without changing its results.

02

Share what’s ready

Pick the part of your process you want to share with your team. Everything else stays in your tool.

03

Talk to the right person

Your team sees the topics you worked on and your name. If something interests them, they can come talk to you.
